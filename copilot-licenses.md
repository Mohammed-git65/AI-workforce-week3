**Copilot Free** is basically the chat style Copilot experience that comes with eligible Microsoft 365 plans. You use it to ask questions, brainstorm ideas, or learn something new, mainly using web information and a small amount of work context. It’s useful if you just want to “talk to AI” occasionally, without needing it inside your everyday work apps.

**Copilot Pro** is more of a personal upgrade to that chat experience. It gives individuals better access and performance, but it’s still focused on conversations rather than deep work integration. It doesn’t handle workflows or business logic, so it’s best for individual users who rely heavily on AI for thinking, writing, or creative work.

**Microsoft 365 Copilot** is very different because it’s built directly into apps like Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. It works securely with your organization’s Microsoft 365 data to help you write emails, summarize meetings, analyze data, and stay productive. Because of this deeper integration, it needs a separate Copilot license and the right permissions. This option is best for business and enterprise users who want AI to actively help them do their daily job inside Microsoft 365.
